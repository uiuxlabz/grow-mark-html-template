Our Story

TerraCorp was founded in 2008 by a team of horticulturists and landscape architects who shared a common vision: to bring world-class green spaces to corporate environments. What started as a five-person team operating out of a small workshop has grown into a 120+ professional organization serving clients across the nation.

Over the past 18 years, we have completed more than 850 projects ranging from corporate campus redesigns to urban agriculture initiatives. Our work has been recognized with 42 industry awards, and we remain at the forefront of sustainable landscaping practices.

We are driven by the belief that nature and business can thrive together. Every project we undertake is an opportunity to create spaces that inspire, restore, and endure.
